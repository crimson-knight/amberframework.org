# Amberframework.org agent instructions

For website copy, documentation, examples, brand visuals, or editorial review,
read and apply [`design/SITE_CONTENT_STANDARDS.md`](design/SITE_CONTENT_STANDARDS.md)
before editing.

When a task requests an adversarial content review:

1. Review the rendered reader journey as well as the source files.
2. Use every blocking rule and the relevant checklist in the content standard.
3. Cite the standard section for each finding.
4. Separate correctness, reader flow, voice, visual identity, and runnable-code
   findings.
5. Record the review and its resolution under `design/reviews/`.

Do not approve a guide because its facts are individually correct. It must also
be understandable in reading order, preserve the documented release boundary,
and leave a reader with a runnable next step.
